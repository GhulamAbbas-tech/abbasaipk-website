About me

Hi, I’m Ghulam Abbas. I’m starting a service to help restaurants, cafés and food businesses improve their online presence and how they communicate with customers.

My aim is simple: help you share your food with more people online, and make it easier for customers to get answers to their questions.

I’m based in Islamabad, Pakistan, and work online with restaurants and food businesses all over the world.

Common questions

Quick answers for restaurant and café owners.

Who do you work with?

I work with restaurants, cafés, bakeries, dhabas and other food businesses anywhere in the world. Everything is done online, so it does not matter which country or city you are in.

Which social media platforms do you help with?

Mainly Facebook and Instagram, the platforms most customers around the world use to find places to eat. I can also prepare content you can share on your WhatsApp status.

What can an AI chatbot answer for my restaurant?

The questions customers ask again and again: opening hours, location, menu items and prices, delivery areas and how to book a table. Questions that need a person are passed to your staff.

Can the chatbot reply in my customers' language?

Yes. It can reply in English, Arabic, Urdu and many other languages, depending on how your customers write.

Do I need any technical knowledge?

No. I set everything up and explain it in simple language, in English or Urdu. You only need to share your menu, timings and photos.

How much does it cost?

It depends on what your restaurant needs. Send me a message on WhatsApp with a few details and I will share a clear price before any work starts.
